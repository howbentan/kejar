A runner and a chaser, near the boxes.

Android, with Kejar open

05

During the chase

Penjara: hold the key

One runner in Penjara, one free runner, one chaser nearby.

5.1

Free runner: scan the Penjara poster

RESCUE ON, the padlock in front of the Penjara gate, 20 s, and a big HOLD button.

5.2

Hold the button

The key turns one click every 5 s with a click sound (rising each time), the marks fill, and the lock shakes harder near the end. Android buzzes on each click.

5.3

Runner in Penjara: watch your screen

The same lock turning, "Hafiz is breaking you out". No button, no sound. Its count matches the rescuer's (within a second).

5.4

Let go for 3 s, then hold again

LET GO, the count dims, "Resets in 3". Holding again keeps the clicks already earned.

5.5

Let go for more than 5 s

The key spins back with the reset sound: "Rescue reset." Scanning again starts from zero.

5.6

During a rescue, another free runner scans Penjara

"Hafiz is already breaking them out." Nothing else changes.

5.7

Rescuer: lock the phone mid-hold

Counts as letting go. Unlock within 5 s and hold again to keep it.

5.8

Rescuer: walk more than 30 m away while holding

The rescue fails: the key snaps. "Nobody gets out this time."

5.9

Chaser: tag the rescuer mid-hold

The rescue fails. Chasers never see the rescuer's name in the feed.

5.10

Hold the full 20 s

The gate swings open, LEPAS! stamps down (within ~3 s of 0), the unlock sound plays. The freed runner reads "You're free. 10 s head start".

5.11

Chaser: scan the freed runner straight away

"Just freed: 7 s head start." No catch. After 10 s, the scan catches.

5.12

Organiser: pause mid-hold, then resume

The count freezes and carries on from the same place.

5.13

After a Mak Kau Hijau that jailed someone

Jailed runners see the chained green gate with "Penjara locked · m:ss". No rescue until it opens.

5.14

Open How to Play from the game

The Penjara card describes the 20 s hold, the 5 s reset, one rescuer and the 10 s head start.
