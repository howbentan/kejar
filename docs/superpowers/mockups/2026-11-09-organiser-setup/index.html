0. Your kit, printed once NEW

Armbands, station cards and the Penjara card are the same in every game, so they print once and get laminated. The secret cards come as a set of three per legendary. Each game uses one the app picks, and a card is reused until someone finds it, then it's retired and you reprint just that one at home. A reprint only counts once you tap "I've printed it". Each player gets 3 wrong scans of a secret card per game, so a photographed copy can't be walked around the zone.
