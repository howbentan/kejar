Bomoh: the hidden-stash card

Two phones, two people. Play it in order with the buttons under each phone: 1 a chaser scans the hidden card and claims it, 2 a runner can see the same mystery zone but can't claim it. Structure reused from 10-dato-seri.html, recoloured purple — Bomoh's own legendary colour — with the real doodle sticker from 2-bomoh-card-art.html.

Reset

1. A chaser's map. A fixed purple zone (same 100–200 m fuzziness as Dato Seri's stash, not yet confirmed for this card), visible all game until claimed.

2. A runner's map, same moment. They see the exact same zone, and can walk over and try — but the scan refuses them. They can still loiter and guard it.
