Bomoh: the doodle sticker, in purple

Same technique as the rest of the deck (13-card-art.html's ART object and filters): a hand-drawn wobbly ink outline, flat real-life-colour fills, a thick white sticker edge. The card frame is purple — Bomoh's own legendary colour, not Dato Seri's yellow — but the character art itself stays in the same skin/cloth/coconut palette as the rest of the cast. Holding the ritual object (the coconut), not posed in any stereotyped gesture, the same dignified treatment the deck's other human figure (Dato Seri's dato card) already gets. Bottom row: at power-up slot size, to check it still reads when small.
