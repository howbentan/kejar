The layout rule: every player screen has the same two rows at the top. First the top bar (44 px): who you are on the left, the clock in the centre, a count on the right. Then the status row, starting 8 px below it: status chips on the left, stacking downwards when there's more than one, and the SOS icon alone on the right, at chip height. Everything keeps the same 12 px side margins as the dock.
Why this spot: the very top edge sits under the phone's status bar and notch, where it's hard to reach and easy to miss. On the status row the icon lines up with the check-in chip, so the row reads as one balanced line rather than a dot squeezed into a corner. The thumb also reaches it more easily.
Unchanged: tap opens the sheet, hold 1 s to send, then the "What's happening?" chips.
