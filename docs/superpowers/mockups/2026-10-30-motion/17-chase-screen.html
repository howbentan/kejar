Before: today, plus this week's additions

Clock row, ping ripple, check-in banner, "Kejar within 100 m" card, a 260 px map, the slot, a separate "Break them out" scanner, 6 feed lines, then SOS. With the slot, snapshot, Dato Seri hint and 5-minute card, the map is pushed half off-screen and the feed needs scrolling. A pocketed runner gets about 10 seconds of attention, so this is too much.

After: Dikejar

Top bar: who you are, the clock, how many are free. Map fills the middle, with small chips on top for the next check-in and "Kejar near". Dock: your slot (tap to use), one Scan for stations, Penjara rescues and the Dato Seri stash (the QR decides which), a one-line ticker (tap for the full feed), and SOS. The check-in banner drops over the top of the map, so the map is still visible below it.

After: Kejar

The same layout, so both sides learn one screen. Chasers keep the page open, so their top chip is "Next ping 1:42" with last-ping age. The big lime button is Scan tag, since catching is the main action. Station claims also go through it. Their slot sits on the left.

The rules behind it (my suggestion)

No scrolling during the chase. Everything fits one screen, on a small Android too (about 360 × 640).

The map is the hero. At least half the screen, always visible, even under the check-in banner.

One Scan button. Stations, Penjara rescue and the Dato Seri stash are all QR codes, and the code says which, so runners don't need three buttons.

Ticker, not a feed list. One line, the newest event. Lines that need you (lime box events) also buzz once. Tap for the full feed in a sheet.

Chips, not cards. Next check-in, "Kejar within 100 m" and the snapshot age are small chips on the map's top edge.

SOS always last and always visible. Nothing ever covers it, the feed sheet included.

One loud thing at a time. If a check-in lands while a sticker pops, the pop waits.
