3. Foil on the legendary only

An iridescent foil layer over the yellow Dato Seri card, with fine lines like a real foil trading card. It shifts as you tilt the phone (or move the mouse). This replaces the plain light sweep. Deck cards never get foil, so the legendary keeps feeling special.

4. Hold to confirm, for marshals

Dangerous marshal buttons fill while held (1.5 s) instead of opening an "Are you sure?" popup, which is easier outdoors with one hand. Letting go early rolls it back. After it's done, an Undo shows for 5 seconds. It's the same idea as the SOS bar, in white, so it never looks like SOS.

5. Loading: the logo traces itself

While the app loads, the marker traces the outline of "kejar", the letters fill white, and the lime full stop pops in last. If loading takes longer, it loops gently. It replaces a spinner on the first open and when joining a game.
