Claiming a power-up, polished, with the marker

Left: the full station flow. Scan, then "Station 3", then five cards fan out. Flick one up (or tap it) within 3 s, it flips, and the marker hypes it before it flies into your slot. Middle: finding Dato Seri, with the same marker in yellow. Right: when a claim is refused. Press Play under each phone.

1. Scan a station, then pick a card

Scan: the viewfinder sweeps, then locks on. A lime "Station 3 ✓" stamp slams in, with DECK · 7 LEFT at the top.
Pick: five cards fan out across the middle with a 3 s bar. Flick one up (or tap it); if you don't, a spotlight sweeps and picks.
Reveal: the card rises, flips, and the marker circles "Got Durian!", adds emphasis marks around the card and draws an arrow down to your slot. Then the card shrinks into the slot, which pulses.

2. Dato Seri: the marker goes yellow

Scanning the hidden stash card skips the five-card fan; there's only one. The yellow ? zone collapses, the foil card bursts in, and the marker draws in yellow: a double loop around the card, stars, burst lines and a crown over the name. It's the only time the marker is ever yellow.
