Organiser setup: five short steps, then walk the park

Today's "New game" is one long page. This splits it into 5 short steps with a progress bar (park, mode, settings, crew, kit). Step 1, the park, is today's saved-parks screen, then an event-day "walk the park" mode that guides you to each spot. Everything on the phones is tappable. Click the cards you like.

Built from your settings: tag count, stations, Penjara, Dato Seri card in Full. Ticking an item draws a marker tick. Ticking everything circles "Ready for event day". Print buttons sit next to what needs printing.

5. Walk the park, place each thing

On event day the app walks you round: the next spot pulses, the card shows distance and direction, and within 15 m it turns lime: "Tape Station 2 here". The Dato Seri stash is last and secret: you choose the exact spot and tap "Drop Dato Seri here". The helper sees the same list, so you can split it.
