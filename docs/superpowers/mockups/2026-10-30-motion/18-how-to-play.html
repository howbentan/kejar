How to play: a short story, per side and per mode

When the organiser taps "Brief the players", every phone opens this. It's 5–7 full-screen cards, like Instagram stories: tap right for next, left for back. Each card has one marker doodle, one headline and one line. The last card is "I'm ready", which ticks you off on the organiser's lobby. Switch side and mode under the phone.
