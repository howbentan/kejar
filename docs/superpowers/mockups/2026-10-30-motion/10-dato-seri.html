Dato Seri in yellow (legendary)

Three phones, three people. Play it in order with the buttons under each phone: 1 a runner sees the hint and finds Dato Seri, 2 the quest-giver grants it, 3 later, in Penjara, Pak Pulis confirms the Settle.

1. Every player's map. A live, fuzzy yellow zone shows roughly where Dato Seri is wandering (100–200 m), all game until someone claims it.

2. The quest-giver's phone. Someone says the phrase in person. The quest-giver picks them and grants Dato Seri, then hands them the coin.

3. Pak Pulis at Penjara. The holder is jailed, shows the coin, and names who else to free. Pak Pulis confirms the Settle.
