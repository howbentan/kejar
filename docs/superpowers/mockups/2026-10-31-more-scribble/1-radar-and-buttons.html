More marker scribble: radar and buttons

Extends 15-marker-scribble.html (§8.5) per §8.10. Four phones: 1 the legendary hint zone's radar sweep replacing the plain ripple (ambient, both colours shown), 2 a proximity radar ping on the chase map (ambient, runners only), 3 the button tick generalised beyond check-in, 4 a new scribble moment for a successful Penjara rescue. Reuses the draw-by-dash-offset technique and helpers from 15-marker-scribble.html.

1. Radar sweep, both legendaries (ambient). A sketchy line rotates around each hint zone, in the zone's own colour, 6 s per turn. Replaces the plain ripple. Stops the instant it's claimed.

2. Proximity radar ping (ambient, runners only). While a chaser is within 100 m, faint scribbled rings pulse outward from your own dot. Stays off the SOS bar — SOS stays clean.

3. The button tick, generalised. Any primary button gets the same small, fast tick check-in already has — here, the chase screen's Scan button and the organiser's "Drop Bomoh here".

4. New moment: a successful rescue. Penjara has no marker moment today — just the lime ring during the 10 s hold. On success, circle "You're free!" and add two stars.
